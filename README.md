# Customer Churn Analysis – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **customer churn** using Power BI. The main objective is to understand how many customers stayed or exited and identify the factors that may be related to customer churn.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains customer information such as:

* Customer ID
* Geography
* Age
* Gender
* Credit Score
* Balance
* Number of Products
* Active Member Status
* Estimated Salary
* Churn/Exit Status

## 📈 Visualizations Created

The following Power BI visualizations were created:

* Stacked Bar Chart
* Clustered Column Chart
* Pie Chart
* Line Chart
* Line and Clustered Column Chart
* Donut Chart
* Matrix
* Donut Chart for retention/churn analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Descriptive Analysis

* Around **79.6% of customers stayed**, while approximately **20.4% exited**.
* **Germany has the highest customer exit rate** compared with France and Spain.
* Customers who exited are generally **older and have higher balances**.

### Diagnostic Analysis

* **Inactive customers have a higher exit rate** than active customers.
* Customers in the **40–60 age group** show a higher tendency to exit.
* Customers with **3 or more products** have a noticeably higher exit rate.

### Predictive Analysis

* Older and inactive customers are more likely to **exit in the future**.
* Customers with multiple products and higher balances may also have a **higher churn risk**.

### Prescriptive Analysis

* The bank should focus on **inactive and older customers** with targeted retention offers.
* Special attention should be given to customers in **Germany**.
* Customers with multiple products should be contacted to understand their concerns and improve retention.

## 🎯 Conclusion

The analysis shows that **age, geography, activity status, and number of products** are important factors associated with customer churn.

The bank can reduce customer exits by identifying high-risk customers early and providing them with **personalized offers, better engagement, and targeted retention strategies**.
